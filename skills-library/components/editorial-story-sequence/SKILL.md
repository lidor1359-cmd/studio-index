# Editorial Story Sequence

## Build

1. Define three to five chapters: origin, craft, product or place, experience, invitation.
2. Give each chapter one short headline, one supporting thought, one strong visual and an optional CTA.
3. Alternate media scale and text position to create rhythm while keeping the system consistent.
4. Reserve animation for chapter transitions and image reveals; keep the rest still.

## Content rules

- Write in short, sensory sentences.
- Let photography and whitespace carry the atmosphere.
- Keep navigation visible but minimal.
- Put practical details such as location, booking and contact at natural moments in the story.

## Guardrails

- Do not use editorial pacing to hide important purchase, accessibility or contact information.
- Optimize large media and provide meaningful alt text.
- Respect reduced motion; every chapter must work as normal content.

## Validate

- The story is understandable if images fail to load.
- Each chapter has one clear purpose and does not repeat the previous one.
